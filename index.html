Quick answers to help you get started with Flowspace.

How does Flowspace help my team work better?

Flowspace brings projects, tasks, and team updates into one shared view, making it easier to stay aligned and focus on the next important step.

Is Flowspace suitable for small teams?

Yes. You can start with a free personal workspace and add projects and teammates as your needs grow.

Can I connect Flowspace with other tools?

Flowspace is designed to fit into your team's workflow. Integrations shown here are part of this product concept demo.

Can I change my plan later?

Absolutely. Choose the plan that fits now and move to another plan as your team changes.

Is my workspace data secure?

This page is a front-end concept demo and does not collect or transmit workspace data.
